# Character Reference Comparison Visual Design

## Style Prompt

Use the supplied `assets/background.png` as the visual anchor: a handmade felt craft room with warm cream fabric, stitched borders, deep teal panels, red plum-flower accents, and soft cozy lighting. The comparison video should feel like a careful handmade lab notebook from "Maki Sunwood ai lab": friendly, tactile, Japanese, and playful, while still presenting the benchmark clearly.

## Colors

- Warm felt cream: `#F3D9AE` for the main canvas and soft label fills.
- Deep stitched teal: `#083B3E` for frames, title bars, and model badges.
- Plum red: `#C7192E` for active accents, scene numbers, and small decorative marks.
- Thread tan: `#C18A55` for dashed stitch borders and secondary lines.
- Ink brown: `#2E2118` for readable Japanese body text.
- Soft ivory: `#FFF2D8` for labels over video and title cards.

## Typography

- Display: `M PLUS Rounded 1c`, heavy weights for warm Japanese labels and headings.
- Data / model labels: `IBM Plex Mono`, tabular, compact and precise.
- Body copy: `M PLUS Rounded 1c`, medium weight.

## Motion

- Motion should feel like paper labels and felt patches being placed on a craft board.
- Use soft slides, small scale-ins, stitch-line draws, and warm light leaks.
- Scene transitions should use cloth-like wipes, blur crossfades, or soft overexposure, not hard digital glitch.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use sterile black tech dashboards.
- Do not use blue/purple SaaS gradients.
- Do not cover the videos with heavy decoration.
- Do not use tiny labels; the final rendered video must be readable.
- Do not introduce a visual style that fights the handmade background image.
